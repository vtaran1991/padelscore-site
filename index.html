PadelScore

Padel scoreboard on your Apple Watch, live on your iPhone by the glass. No ads, no account.

Score from your wrist

Two big tap zones and full padel rules: advantage, golden point or star point, tie-breaks and a super tie-break. Serve indicator and change-of-ends reminders. With workout recording on, the watch stays awake for the whole match and saves it to Apple Health as a workout with heart rate. Scoring needs an Apple Watch.

Your phone is the scoreboard

Lean the iPhone against the court glass: a full-screen live scoreboard with player names and serve indicator follows your watch automatically.

Free to start, Pro to keep playing

Your first 5 matches are free. To keep starting new matches, upgrade to PadelScore Pro: a subscription on Apple Watch or iPhone, or a one-time purchase on iPhone. Everything you have played stays yours: your history and stats remain free, and a match you have started can always be finished and saved.

Purchases and subscriptions

Restore a purchase: in the iPhone app, open Settings (gear icon on the Matches tab), then Pro, then Restore purchase.
Manage or cancel a subscription: in the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. A subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
Purchases are handled by Apple. Terms of Use: Apple Standard EULA.
