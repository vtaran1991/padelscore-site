PadelScore

Padel scoreboard on your Apple Watch. Free — no ads, no subscription, no account.

Score from your wrist

Two big tap zones, full padel rules (golden point, tie-breaks, super tie-break), serve rotation and side-change hints. With workout recording on, the watch stays awake for the whole match and records it as a real workout with heart rate. Scoring needs an Apple Watch.

Your phone is the scoreboard

Lean the iPhone against the court glass: a full-screen live scoreboard with player names and serve indicator follows your watch automatically.

Free, no catch

Scoring, the live scoreboard, match history and stats are free — no limits, no ads, no account, no in-app purchases.
