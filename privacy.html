PadelScore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

PadelScore is built to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. PadelScore has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. We never see your data — there is nothing for us to see.

Where your data lives

  • Match scores and history are stored only on your Apple Watch and iPhone. They sync between your own devices directly (via Apple's WatchConnectivity) and never touch the internet.
  • Heart rate and calories are recorded during a match as an Apple Health workout, on your device, only with your permission. At the end of the match, the average heart rate and active calories for that match are read from Apple Health and saved alongside the score in your match history — on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Your Apple Watch sends this match data (score, and average heart rate/calories) directly to your own iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity, device to device. None of it is sent over the internet, and none of it ever reaches us — we have no servers to receive it.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. PadelScore Pro can be bought on your iPhone and on your Apple Watch. Everything about the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store: Apple shows the prices, takes the payment, and manages your subscription and free trial. We never see your card or payment details, your Apple Account or your name, and we receive no personal information about you. To know whether Pro is active, the app asks Apple's StoreKit framework on your device. That check is between your device and Apple — not us.
  • Free-trial reminder. If you start the free trial, the app can schedule one notification two days before the trial ends. On iPhone, the app asks your permission to send notifications. On Apple Watch, the reminder is delivered quietly, unless you have turned off notifications for the app. It is created and shown by your own device (a local notification). Nothing is sent anywhere for it. If notifications are off, the trial works the same, just without the reminder.
  • Free matches. The number of free matches you have used is kept in a small file on your device. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

What leaves your device

PadelScore has no servers and no code of its own that sends your data over the internet. Scores and match health data (heart rate, calories) travel only from your Apple Watch to your own paired iPhone, directly, over Apple's on-device WatchConnectivity — never over the internet, never to us or anyone else.

The only internet traffic is Apple's own. When you open the Pro screen, buy, or restore a purchase, your device talks to the App Store through Apple's StoreKit. That exchange is between you and Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive or see it. The Terms and Privacy Policy links in the app open in your browser.

Deleting the app deletes all its data (Health workouts remain in Apple Health, under your control, as with any fitness app).

App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected — the strictest label Apple offers, and still accurate: "collected" means data leaving your devices to reach us or a third party, and none of it does. Your match data stays on your own Apple Watch and iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple, and no purchase data reaches us.

Children

PadelScore does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will appear on this page with a new date. The November 5, 2026 update describes PadelScore Pro purchases and subscriptions and the optional free-trial reminder; it does not change what we collect, which is still nothing. Given the app's design (no servers), further meaningful changes are unlikely.
