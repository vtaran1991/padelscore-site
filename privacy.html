PadelScore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

PadelScore is built to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. PadelScore has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. We never see your data — there is nothing for us to see.

Where your data lives

  • Match scores and history are stored only on your Apple Watch and iPhone. They sync between your own devices directly (via Apple's WatchConnectivity) and never touch the internet.
  • Heart rate and calories are recorded during a match as an Apple Health workout, on your device, only with your permission. This data is managed by Apple Health; we never read it back, transmit it, or store it anywhere else.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive no personal information about you.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Deleting the app deletes all its data (Health workouts remain in Apple Health, under your control, as with any fitness app).

App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected — the strictest label Apple offers, and accurate.

Children

PadelScore does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will appear on this page with a new date. Given the app's design (no servers), meaningful changes are unlikely.
